NO PHONE REQUIRED

The same horizon, on your desk.

This is the AR_Horizon field simulator. It loads the same Copernicus pack, the same 3,600-bin horizon and the same label rules as the Android build.

  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click a label.
  • Switch viewpoints to see the Matterhorn from the ridge, the town and the Italian side.
  • Toggle hidden labels: places the ridge blocks are drawn dimmed on the skyline.

The camera and skyline lock are not simulated here. The synthetic lock test lives in tools/validate_skyline_lock.py. The Android app runs the same data without a server.
